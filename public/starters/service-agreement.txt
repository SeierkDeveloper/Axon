SERVICE AGREEMENT

1. Services. The Provider will design and build a marketing website for the Client as described in Schedule A.

2. Fees. The Client pays 12,000 USD: 50% on signing and 50% on delivery. Invoices are due within 7 days. Late
amounts carry interest of 5% per month.

3. Timeline. The Provider will use reasonable efforts to deliver within 8 weeks. Delivery dates are estimates and
the Provider is not liable for any delay, whatever the cause.

4. Changes. Any change to the scope is billed at 150 USD per hour. The Provider alone decides what counts as a
change.

5. Intellectual property. All work product remains the property of the Provider. The Client receives a licence to
use the website that the Provider may withdraw at any time on notice.

6. Liability. The Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to 500 USD. The Client indemnifies the
Provider against all claims arising from the website, without limit.

7. Termination. The Provider may end this agreement at any time without cause. The Client may end it only for
material breach that is not cured within 60 days. Fees paid are not refundable.

8. Renewal. A hosting and maintenance plan of 400 USD per month starts on delivery and renews each year
automatically unless the Client cancels in writing at least 90 days before the renewal date.

9. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the law of the Provider's place of business, and any dispute is
heard there.
